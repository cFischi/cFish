## 3. Resource Allocation Framework

### Time Allocation Guidelines

- **Revenue Generation**: 40% of total time allocation
  - Direct client work and service delivery
  - Business development and sales activities
  - Client relationship management

- **System Development**: 30% of total time allocation
  - Technical implementation of priority systems
  - Documentation improvement and organization
  - Testing and quality assurance

- **Strategic Planning**: 15% of total time allocation
  - Business strategy refinement
  - Partnership development
  - Market research and positioning

- **Administration**: 10% of total time allocation
  - Financial management
  - General administration
  - Business operations

- **Learning & Innovation**: 5% of total time allocation
  - Skill development
  - Research for future capabilities
  - Experimentation with new approaches

### Resource Prioritization Matrix

| Priority Level | Criteria | Example Activities |
|----------------|----------|-------------------|
| P1: Critical | Direct revenue impact within 30 days | Client deliverables, active sales, critical fixes |
| P2: High | Revenue impact within 90 days | Pipeline development, system improvements for key services |
| P3: Medium | Strategic importance, indirect revenue | Documentation improvements, operational efficiency |
| P4: Low | Long-term value, non-urgent | Research, experimental features, nice-to-have improvements |

### Decision Framework

For all resource allocation decisions:

1. **Revenue Impact Assessment**
   - How does this directly impact revenue generation?
   - What is the timeframe for revenue realization?
   - What is the potential return on time invested?

2. **Strategic Alignment Check**
   - How does this align with April 2025 relaunch priorities?
   - Does this support core service offerings?
   - Is this aligned with the Dreamflo philosophy?

3. **Resource Requirement Analysis**
   - What is the total time commitment required?
   - Are there dependencies on external resources?
   - Can this be phased to distribute resource needs?

4. **Opportunity Cost Evaluation**
   - What alternatives would use the same resources?
   - What is the relative priority compared to alternatives?
   - What is the cost of delay or non-implementation?
