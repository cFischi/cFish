## Quick AI Integration Icebreakers (1 minute)

- AI Assistant Introduction: Each participant takes turns asking the [cFish.io](http://cFish.io) AI assistant a creative question to test connectivity and break the ice
- Tech Tool Chain: Share which AI tool in our stack (ClickUp, Notion, Vendasta) you're most excited to learn about and why
- AI Automation Dreams: In one sentence, describe an AI automation you wish existed in your daily workflow
- Platform Personas: If you were an AI assistant, what would your special feature be?
- Integration Inspiration: Share a simple task you hope to automate with our AI tools

Note: Each icebreaker is designed to both engage participants and verify AI tools connectivity as required by the onboarding process.

<aside>
Time Limit: 1 minute per icebreaker activity to align with workshop schedule
</aside>
