# Dreamflo Educational Icebreakers

## Quick Dreamflo Concept Icebreakers (1-2 minutes each)

- **Dream Journey Sharing:** Share one personal or professional dream and map it to the Dreamflo hierarchy (Dream Hive → Mind flo → Theme flo → Step flo)
- **HiveMindZ Numbers:** Pick a number 1-7 from the HiveMindZ framework and explain how that priority level relates to your current goals (Soul, Mind, Body, Produce, Communicate, Connect, or Serve)
- **Symbol Translation:** Choose one Dreamflo symbol (H, M, Z~, z~) and create a quick real-world example of how you'd use it to organize a project or goal
- **Timeline Challenge:** Share one goal and properly categorize it as either Dream Hive (5-10yr), Mind flo (1-3yr), or Step flo (immediate) and explain why
- **Flo State Story:** Describe a time when you were in a perfect "flo" state and connect it to the Dreamflo framework's concept of aligned personal and professional growth

<aside>
These icebreakers help participants understand Dreamflo terminology while sharing meaningful personal connections to the framework
</aside>

Suggested Group Size: 3-8 participants for optimal sharing and learning
