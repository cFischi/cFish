# UcFish Organization Icebreakers

## Subsidiary Spotlight (1-2 minutes)

- **Logo Logic:** Match the subsidiary abbreviation (Fe, Y, W, U, z) with its full name and main function (e.g., "Fe stands for FischEYe, our Production Design Company")
- **Slogan Share:** Explain what "We Uz DSS!" or "Uc, our WebZ P.O.P" means for UcWebZ and the UZ
- **Department Chain:** Connect the seven core departments (OVR → R+D → OPz → PRO → SMC → DMT → SPEC) with their corresponding essence words (Soul, Mind, Body, Produce, Communicate, Connect, Serve)

## Department Deep Dive (1-2 minutes)

- **Number Match:** Match department numbers with their functions (e.g., "If I'm working on 3.2, am I handling Logistics or Resource Allocation?")
- **Department Translation:** Choose a department abbreviation (OVR, R+D, OPz, PRO, SMC, DMT, SPEC) and explain what each letter stands for and its main function
- **Workflow Navigator:** Name a task and identify which department and subdivision it belongs to (e.g., "Where would website maintenance fit: UcWebZ 4.4 or DMT 5.2?")

These icebreakers help team members understand UcFish's organizational structure while making learning interactive and engaging
