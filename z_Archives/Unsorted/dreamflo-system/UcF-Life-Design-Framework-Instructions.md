# UcF ~ Life Design Framework Instructions

## Implementation Instructions

Welcome to the Life Design Framework! This system helps transform your personal vision into actionable steps through a structured, holistic approach to life planning.

## Framework Components

### Life Vision (5-10yr)

Your Life Vision encompasses your ultimate 5-10 year personal aspirations. This is where you create your long-term life goals that will anchor all activities.

Write everything about your life vision, then summarize it into a clear, overarching direction. Your Life Vision should be expressed in two to four sentences that capture your complete personal journey.

### Growth Plan (1-3yr)

Your Growth Plan captures your 1-3 year personal development priorities in one to two clear sentences. This helps prioritize medium-term objectives that directly contribute to your Life Vision.

Your Growth Plan encompasses everything you invest your time and energy into. Use your Life Vision to shape and guide your personal focus for the coming years.

## Life Areas Structure

Your life framework consists of two integrated components:

- **Core Values** - Your fundamental beliefs and long-term aspirations for each life area
- **Current Focus** - Your immediate priorities and growth areas

### Life Areas (1-7)

1. Beliefs/Values: Personal philosophy and spiritual growth
2. Learning/Education: Intellectual development and skills
3. Health/Wellness: Physical and mental wellbeing
4. Wealth/Resources: Financial growth and stability
5. Social/Community: Relationships and social impact
6. Family/Relations: Family connections and personal bonds
7. Purpose/Career: Professional growth and life mission

## Planning Components

### Life Goals

Identify big-picture aspirations that bridge the gap between your current life and your Life Vision. Each Life Goal should be expressed as one clear, measurable objective.

These represent your major life milestones and opportunities that drive personal growth.

### Current Projects

Address immediate life challenges and focus areas that need attention in the coming weeks and months.

During your monthly reflection, identify key projects that align with your Growth Plan.

### Key Milestones

Create specific, measurable achievements for each life area. Milestones should be attainable within 1-4 months.

Each milestone should support and gradually create your Core Values and Life Goals.

### Daily Actions

Break down concrete daily and weekly activities that support your Key Milestones.

Assign specific life areas to each day of the week to ensure balanced personal growth.

## Monthly Life Reflection (10 minutes)

- Review current projects and priorities
- Plan weekly focus areas for the month ahead
- Assess progress on Key Milestones
- Compare results with personal goals
- Adjust course as needed

## Annual Life Review

Once a year, review and update your Life Design Framework:

- Reflect on and refine your 5-10 year Life Vision
- Update your 1-3 year Growth Plan
- Evaluate progress in each Life Area
- Identify gaps between current state and Life Vision
- Set new milestones and daily actions

## Success Metrics

- Goal Achievement: 85-90% completion rate
- Consistency: 90-95% adherence to daily actions
- Life Balance: 85-90% attention to all areas
- Personal Growth: 85-90% progress toward vision

> Your life journey isn't always easy—you'll face challenges, setbacks, and moments of doubt. That's okay. Just stay authentic and honest with yourself. When you find yourself off track, don't worry—simply realign and continue growing.
