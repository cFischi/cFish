### How It Works

1. **Describe Your Vision**: Input a description of your ideal life or business vision. You can be as detailed as you like, but don't worry if you don't have all the details figured out yet.

2. **Generate Your Framework**: Based on your input, the AI will generate a complete Dreamflo framework for you, filling in each section (Dream Hive, Mind flo, HiveMindZ, etc.) with relevant information extracted or inferred from your input.

4. **Download and Share**: Once you are happy with your framework, you can download it in various formats (PDF, Markdown, etc.) and share it with your team, accountability partners, or anyone else!

## Lean Canvas Generator

The Lean Canvas Generator is a specialized AI tool that helps you create a business-focused implementation of the Dreamflo system. It's particularly useful for entrepreneurs and business leaders who want to align their business strategy with the Dreamflo framework.

### How to Use the Lean Canvas Generator

1. **Input Your Business Idea**: Provide a description of your business concept or existing business.

2. **Generate Your Lean Canvas**: The AI will create a Lean Canvas that aligns with the Dreamflo structure, including:
   - Problem/Solution pairs
   - Key Metrics (aligned with HMZ 1)
   - Unique Value Proposition (aligned with HMZ 3)
   - Unfair Advantage (aligned with HMZ 5)
   - Channels (aligned with Dream themes Z)
   - Customer Segments (aligned with Theme Flo Z~)
   - Cost Structure (aligned with Dream steps z)
   - Revenue Streams (aligned with Step flo z~)

3. **Refine and Implement**: Customize the generated canvas and use it as a bridge between your business strategy and Dreamflo implementation.
