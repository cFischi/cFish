# UcF ~ Core Values 2025

This document outlines the fundamental values that guide UcF's operations and culture in alignment with the Dreamflo system implementation for 2025.

## Core Values

- **Live Lovingly FREE & Contagiously HAPPY**
  A central mission statement that drives all operations and decisions across the organization
- **Self-Awareness & Self-Discipline**
  Foundation for personal and business growth through the Dreamflo system
- **Empathy and Inclusivity**
  Fostering diverse perspectives to enhance creativity and innovation
- **Continuous Improvement**
  Commitment to evolution through feedback and experimentation

## Alignment with Operational Excellence

These values directly support the organizational objectives of:

- Operating self-aware, self-disciplined businesses
- Systematic expansion of capabilities and reach
- Clear delineation of responsibilities and functions
- Integration of unified technology stack and automation

## Success Metrics

Values implementation is measured through key performance indicators:

- Session Execution: 90-95% efficiency
- Goal Achievement: 85-90% success rate
- Documentation Quality: 85-90% accuracy
- Overall System Effectiveness: 85-90% optimization
