# Revenue Priority System

## Client Priority Levels

### [REVENUE-CRITICAL]
- New client inquiries from club, lounge, or bar owners
- Follow-up communications for active prospects
- Proposal development for interested clients
- Portfolio requests from potential clients
- Contract discussions with ready-to-close prospects

### [REVENUE-HIGH]
- Portfolio updates for showcase items
- Website enhancements for client contact
- Lead nurturing communications
- Client testimonial collection
- Cross-sell opportunities with existing clients

### [REVENUE-MEDIUM]
- Social media content that showcases portfolio
- Case study development from past projects
- Process improvements for client communication
- Documentation of successful patterns
- Client data management and organization

### [STANDARD]
- Internal process documentation
- Team communication and coordination
- Training and knowledge sharing
- Non-critical system maintenance
- Research and planning activities

### Response Time Commitments
- REVENUE-CRITICAL: 1-4 hours maximum
- REVENUE-HIGH: Same business day
- REVENUE-MEDIUM: Within 24 hours
- STANDARD: Within 48 hours
